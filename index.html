Read the history of Akwete, and who wears it

More than a surface.

Akwete is woven by hand. Extra weft threads are laid in by the weaver to raise the pattern from the ground cloth, so every shape is made, not printed. Look closely and you will see where one hand stopped and the next row began.

At the loom

The loom
A tall upright loom. The Akwete loom is described as the widest in Nigeria, making cloth about 40 to 50 inches wide.
The pattern
Shapes are woven in with extra weft threads over a plain ground cloth, not printed afterwards.
The time
One cloth can take up to three days, depending on its size and how complex the pattern is.
In pairs
Most cloths are woven as a matching pair, worn together: one as the wrapper, the other as a stole, blouse or folded over the top.
